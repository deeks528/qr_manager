Screen 1 MVP Specification

Home (Max 10 Profiles)

Main dashboard featuring quick-access header avatars, active QR card with real-time amount modification, and a compact vertically scrollable list of up to 10 stored profiles.

📌 Key Wireframe Rules:
  • Header displays up to 4 quick profiles + Add (+) button.
  • Tapping avatar or list item instantly switches the active QR card.
  • QR code supports dynamic amount entry without navigating to another screen.
  • Bottom navigation enables one-tap switching to Home, Create, and Settings.
